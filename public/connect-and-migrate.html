Once it's connected, Claude shows a Tool permissions list. Change the dropdown next to Other tools from "Needs approval" to Always allow. This covers all 8 tools at once, so nothing interrupts your interviews with an extra approval popup.

FAQ

Do I have to redo my Voice, Brand, or Local interviews?+
No. Run /migrate in the chat or project where you did them, and Claude reconstructs what you already told it, shows it to you to confirm, and saves it to your account.
What if /migrate can't find something?+
It'll say so plainly instead of guessing or inventing an answer. From there you can run the short interview again — this time it saves to your account for good, not just to that one chat.
Do I still need the old porchlyte-foundations and porchlyte-agents marketplaces?+
No — delete them once you've migrated. They're stuck on an old version for good (a plugin update bug means they never pull new changes), so keeping them around gains you nothing. Worse, leaving both installed means two versions of the same skill can respond to the same request — the new plugin covers everything they did.
Is the Real Estate Trifecta part of this?+
No. It's a separate add-on and keeps working the way it always has, installed on its own from the real-estate-trifecta marketplace.
Do I still need Claude Pro, the desktop app, and the Chrome extension?+
Yes — nothing about your Claude account changes. This upgrade only changes where your profiles live: your PorchLyte account instead of one chat.
What if a slash command doesn't work?+
First, confirm the new plugin is installed and the PorchLyte connector shows as connected under Settings > Connectors. Second, type the full command in a brand new chat. Third, close and reopen the desktop app.
